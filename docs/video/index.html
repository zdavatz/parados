Divided Loyalties — how it works

Three lessons in under two minutes: a bridge scores · the green dilemma · Red + gets cut
🇩🇪 Deutsch 🇬🇧 English 🇯🇵 日本語 🇨🇳 中文 🇺🇦 Українська
1. A bridge scores. A row of three stones from your Colour + can be bridged — 1 point, 2 for a row of four.
2. The green dilemma. Green belongs to Blue + and Yellow +. The green stone Blue plays to finish its own row is the very stone Yellow then scores from.
3. Red + inside your row. Purple serves Blue + but also Red +. A red bridge through it cuts Blue's bridge, which greys out and stops scoring.
